01 / VISION

Digital rights should
move with digital value.

TOG is built around a simple idea: an asset should not lose its rights context when it moves between platforms. A common rights object gives markets, wallets and applications a shared language.

02 / DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Readable first.
Verifiable always.

Rights are only useful when people and software can understand them. Verification states make the boundary between proven information and reported information visible.

03 / ECOSYSTEM

One object.
Many surfaces.

The same profile can appear in a wallet, a marketplace, a lending interface, a creator dashboard or any application that needs to understand what an asset permits.
