01 / THE PROBLEM

Ownership can move.
Rights often do not.

When a digital asset is sold, bridged or moved to another platform, the rights attached to it can lose their context. TOG is designed to make that rights context portable and readable.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

One profile.
Everywhere.

The asset moves. The rights context stays legible. TOG gives applications one shared object to read instead of a different interpretation on every platform.
