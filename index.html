01 / THE PROBLEM

Ownership can move.
Rights often do not.

When a digital asset is sold, bridged or moved to another platform, the rights attached to it can lose their context. TOG is designed to make that rights context portable and readable.

TOG

Put the rights beside the asset.

One profile that wallets, markets and applications can read without rebuilding the rights context from scratch.
