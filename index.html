01 / THE PROBLEM

Ownership can move.
Rights often do not.

When a digital asset is sold, bridged or moved to another platform, the rights attached to it can lose their context. TOG is designed to make that rights context portable and readable.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

One object.
Six signals.

The profile travels with the asset. Applications read the same rights language instead of rebuilding the context from scratch.
